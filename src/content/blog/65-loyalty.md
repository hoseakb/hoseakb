Loyalty is sexy. And beautiful.

In the world of water surface,<br />
Swan and Geese mate for life.<br />
And to know them is to know that<br />
if one dies, the other would die right after<br />
instead of mating with another.

In the world of the endless sky,<br />
Albatrosses mate for life.<br />
No matter how far and long the journey<br />
is, albatross always return to their nest,<br />
to their home, if not... the other pair just die.

In the marine world,<br />
Seahorses mate for life.<br />
And male seahorses would have been<br />
the favourite of feminist: He gets pregnant,<br />
gives birth, and looks after the babies.

In the human world,<br />
there is you, and me.
