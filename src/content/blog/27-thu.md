'Thu'  is a word in Hmar, my mother tongue.<br />
And it means— first of all, the Word.<br />
As in John 1:1-3, "In the beginning..."<br />
A narrative of anything. Poetry. Folk songs.

Second, it also means 'a pungent smell',<br />
from fart, shit, etc... to any other thing<br />
that smell unpleasant.<br />
Third, it also refers to the four main<br />
Hmar kitchen curry condiments— sathu, sithu,<br />
ngathu  and bekanthu. Traditionally fermented<br />
pork lard, sesame seeds, fish, and soyabean.

I come from a world where just one lexical word<br />
'thu'  can mean almost everything I love<br />
about my culture. And so, why wouldn't my taste,<br />
and my sensibility, should not be any different?

'Thu', therefore, is the beginning of everything,<br />
and shall be the last. It can create, build,<br />
and destroy.
