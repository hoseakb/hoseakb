In the world where I am but just one<br />
of the many grandchildren,<br />
in the world where I have two sets<br />
of grandparents— paternal and maternal,<br />
both are equally important with their prerequisites<br />
Hmar traditional roles in my life as a grandchild.<br />
Paternal surname, I inherit.<br />
Maternal surname goes under the label 'puhai',<br />
maternal side of the lineage, the darker side<br />
of the moon. Or sometimes, the brighter side.

Yesterday, my paternal grandmother, who lived<br />
a full life of 94 years on earth, passed away.<br />
And today, her mortal remains were buried<br />
in the cemetery in Parbung, my ancestral village.

I only knew her as Pipi Laltling (grandma Laltling),<br />
the mother of my father, and when I got to know that<br />
her full name has always been 'Laltlingzo',<br />
I felt as if I was a failure as a grandchild.<br />
But then, I also know that my grandmother<br />
never knew me as 'Hosea', because my grandmother<br />
is just my grandmother. For instance,<br />
She also would not know that I write poetry.

So, my grandmother is my grandmother,<br />
the mother of my father, just as my mother<br />
shall be the grandmother of my child.<br />
And now that she is no longer here,<br />
I begin to feel something that is almost<br />
like a traditional grief.
