Thina thlânkhar tuorem hnuoiah,<br />
ril anga deiin piellei lai sam ang zal ta la khawm,<br />
lungriemin a mi tlâk châng hin lungrila sam ang ṭhe<br />
rêng rel hih a lan tak hlak ie maw, Pi.

Lungdithai lo kai hmasakna, lungzuorhai lêngna<br />
varâlrama chun, ka pu leh in inhmu ta am?<br />
Ni Din le Ni Hrangawizo hai le te?<br />
U Ramhnienglo le Papui Vunga hai te?<br />
Chatuon hun kim vawngtu'n a bawl zo ta sa,<br />
Vânin inhawi chawrsawnglêra chun,<br />
chatuon thlifim indawng za ta am?

Chahnung hmapût um ta nawna ram pana,<br />
kumtluonga hadam dinga pângmuol khûm ni ta<br />
inla khawm, mihriem inthlakhla dan hlak hin<br />
chinlem a nei si nawh. In ṭinsan hnung khawvel hi<br />
a la ruok zuol pei awm hih.

Favâng boruok khawm an thieng ta a.<br />
Tukram lozau hai khawm an mawi lai tak a nih.<br />
Zân ruosur khawm imu a suk dên a.<br />
Zotuithieng luongna vadungtea hai khawm<br />
ai an suok ṭan ta. Sienkhawm,<br />
Zamuol in liem san hnung khawvel hi a mawi<br />
famkim zo ta nawh.
