By now, you have become solid,
that state of matters we learned about
in our high-school chemistry class,
that state of matters I like most—solid state,
not in your state of air, nor liquid,
but solid, tangible, touchable, feel-able.
Because I like to feel it solidly what belongs
with me.

And so, I don't know if you know this,
but with you, I have questioned my status in life.
I have questioned my social standing.
I have questioned my life before you, all of it.
Because now that you are here, now that it's life
after you, life after I met you, this is finally,
this is nothing short of whatever I have always
prayed for.
It's the end of September and I know, I know
but I am not anxious at all for the first time.
Because you are here, finally.
You are here with me.
Autumn is beautiful, at long last, for me.
Because of you.
