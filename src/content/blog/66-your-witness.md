If ever my love should not be enough<br />
for me to be in your life forever,

If ever my love should not be enough<br />
to be the reason of your life-altering<br />
decision in this lifetime,

If ever my love should not be enough<br />
to be the light in your dark night,

Please know that I have already given<br />
all my heart, body, prayers, and wishes<br />
to be your one and only witness to life<br />
for the rest of this lifetime.
