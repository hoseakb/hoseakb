The other day, my childhood friend<br />
whom I haven't kept in touch with for years<br />
called me on the phone and said,<br />
"Bai, indu taluo tah."<br />
(My friend, you have become very indu)

Indu is an attribute you label someone<br />
who is aloof, distant to the point of<br />
appearing self absorbed.<br />
But it doesn't mean prideful, nor boastful<br />
that is 'inti thei'.

I mean, when my friend called me 'indu'<br />
I couldn't reply to him properly<br />
because indeed, I haven't been the same<br />
person whom he knew back in the days.<br />
I have read many books, I have obsessed<br />
over fictional characters, hip-hop artists,<br />
pop stars, movie stars.<br />
I have been to many places encountering<br />
many people.

And I have been all these, or maybe all these<br />
have become a part of me.<br />
And now, if someone who knew me before<br />
called me 'indu', maybe I am, maybe I am not.<br />
And that's poetry, the very matter of life.
