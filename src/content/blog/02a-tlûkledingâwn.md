It is a quantifying word in Hmar,<br />
an equivalent for billion, a number<br />
with 9 zeroes. And probably the least use<br />
word in the lexical world of the Hmar,<br />
my native tongue. Because my culture<br />
has never been economically wealthy enough<br />
to require the word on a daily.

The word 'Tlûkledingâwn'  is not only<br />
dramatically useless but also hyperbolically<br />
hilarious as in 'Tlûk'  for crashing down,<br />
'le'  for and, and 'dingâwn'  for bending down<br />
with weight.  Therefore, Tlûkledingâwn<br />
dramatically and hyperbolically means<br />
bending and crashing down under a great weight.

And so I estimate everything in my world<br />
and also everything I have ever owned.<br />
I compare my sum load of everything together<br />
with your weight in my heart<br />
only to find out that you are the only worth<br />
equivalent to Tlûkledingâwn  that exist<br />
in my world. You are my Tlûkledingâwn.
