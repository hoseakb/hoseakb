If I should begin from the beginning,<br />
that is, way before you came to cup lovingly,<br />
in your palm, my heart, with love<br />
and tenderness— you see, even now<br />
I don't know whatever that was that<br />
I have done good in this life to deserve you...<br />
So, I cannot just make myself believe that<br />
I deserve this— I deserve you, simply—<br />
that perchance I have done accidental good deeds<br />
to deserve you.

And so, God tells me otherwise.<br />
That— June will never be the same<br />
and that the rain, the down-pouring rain,<br />
in our Indian monsoon season<br />
will never be the same. Because YOU<br />
happened...

So, whenever you pray, Ṭhamâmpui,<br />
whenever you pray with me,<br />
the angels who have been assigned for me<br />
in Heaven always go crazy... Because<br />
you are here with me on Earth.<br />
And I know that you are here to stay<br />
for a lifetime.
