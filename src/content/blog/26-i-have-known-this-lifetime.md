I have known this lifetime
only as far as my own days allowed.
I have known Parbung, my ancestral village,
only as far as my childhood reaches.
And the world beyond Parbung
I have known it too,
but narrowly, strangely, subjectively.

I have been known by others
just as I have known them—
fragmented, partial, incomplete.

I have known this lifetime like this,
like a half moon:
one way, one-sided, unfinished.

And then, you happened.
The rising of my June full moon.

You happen—and it is everything.
Now that I have known you,
now that it's September, I know enough
of this life and its timeline— sufficiently, fully,
just as I am always meant to.
