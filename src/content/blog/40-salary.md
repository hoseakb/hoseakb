I want to blame Sir Adam Smith,
and Karl Marx partly,
for my 21st century economic awareness.
And thus, this restless self dissatisfaction.

I earn 29 thousands Indian rupees per month
with my job, this is 326.92 US dollar internationally,
by working rain or shine daily for a month.
And 80% of this salary vanishes before my payday
just to barely sustain the need and basic requirements
of modern day 2025 daily existence,
even in a small town like where I live and love,
and dream, and aspire.

I don't know what it is exactly and concretely
but by being a salary-earning member of society,
I feel like I have become less societal, less churchy,
less passionate, less... less than anything
whom I have always dreamed I ought to be.
