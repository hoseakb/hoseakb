1 John 4:8 says, "God is love."
And I have no doubt about it
nor this poem, my gosh! It is never
about love, nor me.
It is about love according to St. John.

Because I know that my love is
a jealous thing.
And loyal:
I shall never ever do anything
that will betray your position in my heart
and soul and that,
now that I have given myself
to you, No! not you, but you're everlasting
charm on the Earth and heaven. I have become a neck you can
cut in any bloody ways.
