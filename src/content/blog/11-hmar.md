There exists a larger world inside me,<br />
way larger than the physicality of myself,<br />
the actual extent of which I do not know.<br />
And this world that only stays within the confine<br />
of the interiority of my conscious<br />
and subconscious mind only speak<br />
and dream in Hmar, the language of my birth.

On that December night when I was born<br />
my mother sang me to sleep with her native lullaby<br />
of a brooding hen which she learned from her mother.<br />
And my father, same as the rest of my maturing life,<br />
remained drunkenly silent throughout the night.<br />
Only later in life did I get to find out that<br />
he was like that only because he had always been<br />
half illiterate. From there, from that night<br />
I was born I began learning how to speak,<br />
how to articulate myself to the rest of the world.<br />
First, to the ashes of my mother's traditional hearth.<br />
And only then did I learn how to decipher the wordings<br />
in my father's silence.

And now, even though I have spent all my life learning<br />
how to find poetry in the language of this poem,<br />
I shall always speak best and most intimately,<br />
in the language of my interior world.<br />
That is, Hmar, the language of my birth.
