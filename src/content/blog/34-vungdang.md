I shall never go and blame the fault
in my stars, nor my parents,
not my childhood, nor my ancestors...
because everything is set for life now
for me. Whatever biblical karma, or the covenant
punishment of 7 generations there might be.
Only the blood of Jesus, and the grace of Heaven
shall ever redeem me from me.
And I have been redeemed.
I had committed blasphemy, repeatedly.
I had attempted to kill myself.
I had committed sins, repeatedly.
I had doubted my faith. I had stopped praying.
I had blamed the Heaven whenever misfortune
arrived at my doorstep. I had done wrongs.

Maybe that's why I love poetry so much.

And in all these, all through these—
I have been redeemed by the virtue of Holy blood.
I am redeemed. And I know I am loved.
And no generational curse of any family
shall do me harm. Because I am redeemed.
I am loved. I am now known by my name.
