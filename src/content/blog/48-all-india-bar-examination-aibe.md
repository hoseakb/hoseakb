I live in India, specifically in the Northeast
state of Manipur— after 3rd May demarcation,
in one of the hilly districts capital— the town
of many names: Lamka/Tuithraphai/Churachandpur.
And the only time that people like me to have ever
come close to the department of the Indian Justice system
is when one of the neighborhood boys gets themselves
into the Manipur Police Force. Or IRB.

What I am saying is: even after 16+ degree certificates
from the Indian Educational System, I would not know
with whom and where to go if I ever want to apply for FIR.

What if my father is killed in the most unjustified manner?
Will he be labeled a Tribal terrorist, as one
of the perpetrators of a terrorist encounter?

The answer, my friend, is only among the hills,
because my father— he is, and always will be
 just a half-illiterate day laborer.
