In Hmar, night dreams that come to you<br />
when you sleep is simply 'Mang', but in poetry<br />
one way of saying it is 'Tawnmang',<br />
'tawn' to experience, encounter. So, 'Mang'<br />
is not simply a dream.<br />
It is an experience you encounter<br />
on the other side<br />
of your day.

And you know how it is,<br />
there are mornings when you wake up<br />
with a certain dream and its after-smoke<br />
heavily lingers on your eyelid long after.

Maybe this is why when you say goodnight<br />
traditionally in Hmar, you say "Mangṭha",<br />
bidding good dream.<br />
Maybe because I slept last night with your<br />
before sleep 'Mangṭha', my 'Mang' was good,<br />
and now I want to see you again. I want you.<br />
I want to sit beside you. I want you. I yearn.
