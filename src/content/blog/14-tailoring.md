By afternoon, I would watch
your scissors cutting through cloths.
Every movement is a poem.
And then you'd ask,
"What's so fascinating about it?"
It is love, your love being audible
in the quiet, the unsaid part of things.

Because all at once,
I would know, once again,
you love me that way.
