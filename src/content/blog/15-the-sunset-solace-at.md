I do not know when you become<br />
the quiet peace-giving part of my day,<br />
the sunset solace at the end of my day,<br />
but you do indeed, and you have become<br />
the one place I run to whenever I need<br />
to get away from the noises... of my day.

I have not really known silence before<br />
especially the peaceful kind. I grew up<br />
in a typical Hmar household,<br />
and the neighborhood... And more<br />
noisier yet is the inside of my head,<br />
and the kuduk kuduk beatings<br />
in the hollow cavity of my chest,<br />
and the never-ending market-like noises<br />
of my mind.

For instance, today<br />
my day was draining and noisy,<br />
so by afternoon, I was drained out<br />
of clear eyesight and clear head.<br />
And you, you were there...

You and the loud language you always<br />
manage to speak with your eyes<br />
and that whenever I get to sit beside you,<br />
even on a drained out evening like today,<br />
I would always feel like I am finally at rest<br />
at long last, where I am peacefully supposed to be.
