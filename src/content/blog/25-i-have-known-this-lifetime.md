I have known this lifetime<br />
only as much as my own life has permitted me:<br />
I have known Parbung, my ancestral village,<br />
only as much as my childhood days had allowed me.<br />
And the rest of the world outside of Parbung,<br />
also only as much, limitedly and subjectively.

I have also been known to people<br />
just as I have known them, limitedly and subjectively.

I have known this lifetime, only as much,<br />
only halfway, only one way, only one sided.

And then, you happened.<br />
You happen, and it's everything.<br />
And so, now that I have known you,<br />
I know this lifetime enough as much<br />
as I should and must.
