My dreams are outrageously funny
sometimes. At other times,
I would wake up to some particular dream
still lingering heavily on my eyelids:

The other day, last month I think,
I woke up to this terrorizing dream.
I knew it was just a dream but
for five minutes, in the first hour
of that morning, I was paralyzed with
this sickening, vomit inducing high temperature—
in my dreams, I saw you riding around town
with your ex. You were talking to him,
texting him... In my dream.

You couldn't help yourself.
I was not enough. You didn't love me enough
to cut your ex off completely.

But by the afternoon of that day,
when I saw your face... I knew that it was all
a dream...that my dream of the night
was just a dream, the psychological effects
of fearing to lose someone you love so completely.
