It is one of those afternoons
in late summer when sunset
flirt with whatever room you are in
through a curtained window
in the way early autumn sunset flirt.

It is the last class of the day
and my students are sweating out answers
to a bunch of questions on Edgar Alan Poe.

The day has been sweltering
and besides that, anything special is yet
to happen still in my side of the world.
And then, you send me a package of you,
your thoughts, and the longing of your heart
through internet. God bless the Internet!

And now I am yearning for you from where I am,
the afternoon look of your face, your fragrance.
