I met him in NEHU,
among the pine trees, hostel poverty,
and winter cold

At first, I thought he was a Shillong boy
because he spoke English with this distinct
accent the way most of my limited Shillong
friends speak. It turned out he was not,
but that's not the point... Yesterday, I saw
his life update on Instagram, and he is engaged now!
Ban Kurbah, my dearest friend, is getting married!

In 2022 I think, we were on the phone
and he told me he read my poems to his students.
I was grinning ear to ear that day.

He was there, when I found my poetry
in Shillong. And this, this is a poem
remembering him because he was the one person
who explained 'U Thlen' story
and its Khasi connotation when I was curious.
