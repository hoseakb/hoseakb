Eve, in that eternal paradise garden of Eden,
surely linked up with the serpent before. Before,
before that day she got entangled in the webs
of Satan. She knew him before. She must have.
Satan must have been her ex, in all honesty.

Haunted house. It's scary.
It's the one place almost all horror movies
take place. Anything, a place, or a person
that is haunted by the ghost of the past,
the old past that is supposed to be bygone,
 it's scary. It's toxic. It's damaging.

Adam, that time when his wife Eve
betrayed him with the serpentine Satan,
even though he knew about it all clearly,
because he loves his wife Eve
and that he thought he couldn't live
without her, without her he is not Adam,
and so he ate the fruit of eternal damnation.
Because it's either Eve or eternal loneliness?

And the horror of the aftermath,
it's scary. It's toxic. I fear, heart-crashingly.
