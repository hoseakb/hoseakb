The other day, my childhood friend
whom I haven't kept in touch with for years
called me on the phone and said,
"Bai, indu taluo tah."
(My friend, you have become very indu)

Indu is an attribute you label someone
who is aloof, distant to the point of
appearing self absorbed.
But it doesn't mean prideful, nor boastful
that is 'inti thei'.

I mean, when my friend called me 'indu'
I couldn't reply to him properly
because indeed, I haven't been the same
person whom he knew back in the days.
I have read many books, I have obsessed
over fictional characters, hip-hop artists,
pop stars, movie stars.
I have been to many places encountering
many people.
And I have been all these, or maybe all these
have become a part of me.
And now, if someone who knew me before
called me 'indu', maybe I am, maybe I am not.
And that's poetry, the very matter of life.
