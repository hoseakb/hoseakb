I shall never go and blame the fault<br />
in my stars, nor my parents,<br />
not my childhood, nor my ancestors...<br />
because everything is set for life now<br />
for me. Whatever biblical karma, or the covenant<br />
punishment of 7 generations there might be.<br />
Only the blood of Jesus, and the grace of Heaven<br />
shall ever redeem me from me.<br />
And I have been redeemed.

I had committed blasphemy, repeatedly.<br />
I had attempted to kill myself.<br />
I had committed sins, repeatedly.<br />
I had doubted my faith. I had stopped praying.<br />
I had blamed the Heaven whenever misfortune<br />
arrived at my doorstep. I had done wrongs.

Maybe that's why I love poetry so much.

And in all these, all through these—<br />
I have been redeemed by the virtue of Holy blood.<br />
I am redeemed. And I know I am loved.<br />
And no generational curse of any family<br />
shall do me harm. Because I am redeemed.<br />
I am loved. I am now known by my name.
