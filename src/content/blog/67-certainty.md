When I talked about you
with my mother, she said,
"She must really be the woman
of my prayers. Please bring her home,
I want to see her face."

Last week, I had this dream
in which I saw you walking hand in hand
with someone who was your past or whom
who you seem to like a lot.
But when I told you about it, my dream,
I omitted the identity of the man.
And half of my dream, including the part
where I woke up to see my body all sweaty,
fast breathing, and images of my dream.

Toi, don't you know that I am so so afraid
that you will not choose me in the end?
That you'll talk with someone else
and find me suddenly insufficient?
And that, if this ever be the case,
I and my love won't just be enough
to do anything about it.
