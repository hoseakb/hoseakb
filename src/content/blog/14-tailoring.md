By afternoon, I would watch<br />
your scissors cutting through cloths.<br />
Every movement is a poem.<br />
And then you'd ask,<br />
"What's so fascinating about it?"<br />
It is love, your love being audible<br />
in the quiet, the unsaid part of things.

Because all at once,<br />
I would know, once again,<br />
you love me that way.
