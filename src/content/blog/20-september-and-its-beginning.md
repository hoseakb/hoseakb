There is this old sadness<br />
in the old 2010s Bollywood songs<br />
that, sometimes, comes crashing into me<br />
like a childhood Parbung nostalgia.

I am on my way to work,<br />
and to drown out the mad poetry<br />
of this town morning traffic, I put on<br />
my headphones. Just before I set off from home,<br />
my mother got a phone call that shocked her<br />
to tears— Nu Khuongdailo has passed away.

And all these, with the added heaviness<br />
of Monday, of the new month, of the new season,<br />
and the continued requirement to make a living<br />
despite the dust of Muolvaiphei road<br />
and the heat of the sun should have been enough<br />
to crash me completely but you, you,<br />
you have made it easy, you have made me...<br />
you have made it... you, you have made it bearable.

Because with you in my life, even the heavy beauty<br />
of September and its beginning does not scare me<br />
for the first time.
