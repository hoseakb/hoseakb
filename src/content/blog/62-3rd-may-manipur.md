Today this year 2026 is a Sunday<br />
and I should be feeling holy and churchy<br />
but it's been 1,096 days since 3rd May 2023<br />
when I had to leave the capital city<br />
of my home state in terror. I am not alright.<br />
It's been 3 years<br />
and the nightmares of 3rd May continue.

Many people died on May 3rd Manipur<br />
and many more houses burnt, displaced,<br />
and traumatized. Sometimes, I think<br />
the grudges of their ghosts are haunting us<br />
now.

3rd May, it's been a long long torture<br />
and I condemn you!
