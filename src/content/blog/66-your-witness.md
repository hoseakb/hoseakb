If ever my love should not be enough
for me to be in your life forever,
If ever my love should not be enough
to be the reason of your life-altering
decision in this lifetime,
If ever my love should not be enough
to be the light in your dark night,
Please know that I have already given
all my heart, body, prayers, and wishes
to be your one and only witness to life
for the rest of this lifetime.
