I would not know how the stars are
tonight in the sky above my dusty
shivering Muolvaiphei street.
I am in my room, summered and without you,
and in front of me are piles and piles
of academic works.

So, Toi,
this is me thinking of all the lovely things
to get me through the night, dusty
April February nights without you.
Because the thoughts of you on my home
is not you, nor the nightly
on my legs, nor this December hoodie.

You must be here. You must be here.
You need to be all the warmth in my nights
because, now that you exist, no warmth
is warm sufficiently enough, unless it's you.
