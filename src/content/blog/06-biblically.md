1 John 4:8 says, "God is love."<br />
And I have no doubt about it<br />
nor this poem, my gosh! It is never<br />
about love, nor me.<br />
It is about love according to St. John.

Because I know that my love is<br />
a jealous thing.<br />
And loyal:

I shall never ever do anything<br />
that will betray your position in my heart<br />
and soul and that,<br />
now that I have given myself<br />
to you, No! not you, but you're everlasting<br />
charm on the Earth and heaven.<br />
I have become a neck you can<br />
cut in any bloody ways.
