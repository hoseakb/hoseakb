I met him in NEHU,<br />
among the pine trees, hostel poverty,<br />
and winter cold

At first, I thought he was a Shillong boy<br />
because he spoke English with this distinct<br />
accent the way most of my limited Shillong<br />
friends speak. It turned out he was not,<br />
but that's not the point... Yesterday, I saw<br />
his life update on Instagram, and he is engaged now!<br />
Ban Kurbah, my dearest friend, is getting married!

In 2022 I think, we were on the phone<br />
and he told me he read my poems to his students.<br />
I was grinning ear to ear that day.

He was there, when I found my poetry<br />
in Shillong. And this, this is a poem<br />
remembering him because he was the one person<br />
who explained 'U Thlen' story<br />
and its Khasi connotation when I was curious.
