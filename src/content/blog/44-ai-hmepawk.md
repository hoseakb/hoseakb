"Ai"  is a river crab
that can be found fat and tasty in autumn
in this part of the world, in the hills, where I live.
"Hmepawk" is the traditional style
how we cook autumn crabs with paddy rice
and green bean leaves. It's the taste of autumn.

There are dishes so dear to my taste palate
that I just cannot explain how it tastes fully
other than in my mother tongue, Hmar.

That's how I love you. No words
in my dictionary, both English and Hmar,
would ever do justice to how much,
how completely, how fully
