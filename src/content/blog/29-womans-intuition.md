I have known about it from the best,
my mother— with her, mostly it has been
always about my father.

Some morning, she would wake up
with her bad dreams, "Mang Sie"
as she would call it. And whatever premonition
carries by her dream, it would always, it would
always come true to the dot.
There are other instances when she would know
exactly what's going on even if I avoid telling her.

And now that you belong with me, Ṭhamâmpui,
would you be like my mother?
Would your intuition be as spooky, knowing
the weather of my daily landscape even when
I do not have the courage to tell you out of inphalam?
