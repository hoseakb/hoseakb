It takes me thirty three autumns,<br />
or maybe a lot more, to finally find<br />
the one, the woman with whom I want<br />
to have children and create a family.<br />
To create a family is nothing like<br />
creating a poem. Especially<br />
in this modern days, where<br />
even merely existing is scary.

I am, and I was, a product<br />
of my own mother's motherhood.<br />
Even after a lifetime of MA degree<br />
in English Literature, hundreds of lifestyle<br />
magazines, novels, 4 epic poems,<br />
hundreds of movies, the Internet,<br />
Tolkien, Rowling, Mario Puzo, Pablo Neruda,<br />
Mahmoud Darwish, Rainer Maria Rilke,<br />
academic conferences, PhD research...

I am, and I am still the product of my mother's<br />
Hmar motherhood. Her language becomes<br />
my mother's tongue. And her love...<br />
the only kind of love I might truly understand.

So, Ṭhamâmpui,<br />
when I say I love you, I love you in Hmar,<br />
with whatever my mother's traditional motherhood<br />
has made me. That is, nakedly and with everything<br />
I am in this life and on Earth, with everything<br />
I shall ever be.
