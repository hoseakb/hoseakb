There is something in listening<br />
to the gurgling of rain water<br />
behind the window of your room at night.<br />
It's September, and I don't remember<br />
which and what day... because I only know you.

And it's raining right now, and outside<br />
my window is a world where I will be drenched<br />
if I ever go out. I miss you, I miss you<br />
whenever it rains, I miss you in the rain,<br />
at nights like tonight, like right now.

You have done what September rain<br />
does to a night like tonight, like right now,<br />
like the gurgling sound of a roof rainfall<br />
behind my window, just like that, just like that<br />
to me.
