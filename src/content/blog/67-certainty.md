When I talked about you<br />
with my mother, she said,<br />
"She must really be the woman<br />
of my prayers. Please bring her home,<br />
I want to see her face."

Last week, I had this dream<br />
in which I saw you walking hand in hand<br />
with someone who was your past or whom<br />
who you seem to like a lot.<br />
But when I told you about it, my dream,<br />
I omitted the identity of the man.<br />
And half of my dream, including the part<br />
where I woke up to see my body all sweaty,<br />
fast breathing, and images of my dream.

Toi, don't you know that I am so so afraid<br />
that you will not choose me in the end?<br />
That you'll talk with someone else<br />
and find me suddenly insufficient?<br />
And that, if this ever be the case,<br />
I and my love won't just be enough<br />
to do anything about it.
