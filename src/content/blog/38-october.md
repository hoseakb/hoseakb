In Hmar, my mother tongue, October
is known as 'Chang-er'.
And I am wondering why I have never
thought about October
from that point of view. Maybe because
October has always been just October,
before you, plainly and heavily,
before you changed it all.

'Chang-er' comprises of two words,
'chang' meaning paddy rice,
'er' meaning dry cooking.
'Chang-er', therefore, means that season
when you can puff up newly harvested
paddy rice by heating up in the fireplace.
This is to mean, all together, 'Chang-er' means
the season of the first paddy rice harvest.

You see, now that you are here,
now that you are here in this time of the year,
with me... October, by default,
couldn't just be the same. It's a personal season
of maturity, harvest, beautiful hills, sunsets,
and you. And you. October with you, now.
