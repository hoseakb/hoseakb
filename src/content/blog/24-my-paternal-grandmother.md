In the world where I am but just one
of the many grandchildren,
in the world where I have two sets
of grandparents— paternal and maternal,
both are equally important with their prerequisites
Hmar traditional roles in my life as a grandchild.
Paternal surname, I inherit.
Maternal surname goes under the label 'puhai',
maternal side of the lineage, the darker side
of the moon. Or sometimes, the brighter side.

Yesterday, my paternal grandmother, who lived
a full life of 94 years on earth, passed away.
And today, her mortal remains were buried
in the cemetery in Parbung, my ancestral village.

I only knew her as Pipi Laltling (grandma Laltling),
the mother of my father, and when I got to know that
her full name has always been 'Laltlingzo',
I felt as if I was a failure as a grandchild.
But then, I also know that my grandmother
never knew me as 'Hosea', because my grandmother
is just my grandmother. For instance,
She also would not know that I write poetry.

So, my grandmother is my grandmother,
the mother of my father, just as my mother
shall be the grandmother of my child.
And now that she is no longer here,
I begin to feel something that is almost
like a traditional grief.
