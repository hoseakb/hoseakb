Ab Samsui is a poet who writes<br />
mostly in Hmar, my mother tongue.<br />
In one of his poems, he writes about<br />
the rustic beauty of his beloved's hands,<br />
marred by an eldest daughter's responsibilities<br />
and Pherzawl village jhumfield works.

Toi, while reading the poem I thought of<br />
your right-hand thumb. The harden knuckle<br />
right above your thumb because of your long-term<br />
scissors handling. And your bones, subjected under<br />
a constant constraint of deadline orders.

Toi, when I say, "You are pretty. (I hmel a thra.)"<br />
I truly mean it along with the intricacies of the poetry<br />
of all your designed-and-tailored dresses.
