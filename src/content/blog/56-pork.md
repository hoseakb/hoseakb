Don't you know that I have found<br />
traces of you, your solidness<br />
in the appetizing taste<br />
of my traditionally cooked,<br />
in my winter pork curry, Naute?<br />
Winter is going to be cold.

It's December<br />
and tender mustard leaves are on sale<br />
in the market, and ginger bulbs<br />
and bamboo fermented shoot.<br />
Come home soon, Naute.<br />
Come home and let me cook you<br />
the tastiest of pork recipes<br />
I have learned how to cook.
