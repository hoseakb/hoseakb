I have known this lifetime<br />
only as far as my own days allowed.<br />
I have known Parbung, my ancestral village,<br />
only as far as my childhood reaches.<br />
And the world beyond Parbung<br />
I have known it too,<br />
but narrowly, strangely, subjectively.

I have been known by others<br />
just as I have known them—<br />
fragmented, partial, incomplete.

I have known this lifetime like this,<br />
like a half moon:<br />
one way, one-sided, unfinished.

And then, you happened.<br />
The rising of my June full moon.

You happen—and it is everything.<br />
Now that I have known you,<br />
now that it's September, I know enough<br />
of this life and its timeline— sufficiently, fully,<br />
just as I am always meant to.
