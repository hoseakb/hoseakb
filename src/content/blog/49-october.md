It's the last day of the month,
October is ending. And there shall be
no other day exactly like today
and I need you, dearly. I need you
as you were today. Now, here, now.
I mean, this is what my heart desires
and against it
is my sense of reality. I am old enough,
mature enough to know that
one doesn't always get one's desires.
There had been days like today
and there shall be more.

It's the ending day of October
and the paddy rice field near my house
are all harvested since yesterday.
And I haven't seen your face today.
