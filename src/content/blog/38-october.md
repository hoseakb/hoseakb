In Hmar, my mother tongue, October<br />
is known as 'Chang-er'.<br />
And I am wondering why I have never<br />
thought about October<br />
from that point of view. Maybe because<br />
October has always been just October,<br />
before you, plainly and heavily,<br />
before you changed it all.

'Chang-er' comprises of two words,<br />
'chang' meaning paddy rice,<br />
'er' meaning dry cooking.<br />
'Chang-er', therefore, means that season<br />
when you can puff up newly harvested<br />
paddy rice by heating up in the fireplace.<br />
This is to mean, all together, 'Chang-er' means<br />
the season of the first paddy rice harvest.

You see, now that you are here,<br />
now that you are here in this time of the year,<br />
with me... October, by default,<br />
couldn't just be the same. It's a personal season<br />
of maturity, harvest, beautiful hills, sunsets,<br />
and you. And you. October with you, now.
