Your face is a landscape with its own raw poetry<br />
in its entirety, with its own hills and running valley,<br />
with its own moon phases of the zodiac seasons,<br />
with its own rising and falling of weather in the sun,<br />
and indeed, God must be very pleased.

Because your face is a landscape with its own pristine<br />
Hmar poetry and I, I cannot be anything else now<br />
except to be yours—to be your most devoted poet<br />
for I want to make poetry out of it just as the moon<br />
makes seasons out of a year's landscape.

And to witness your lips, naked or red lipstick stained,<br />
your lips and its movement of opening or closing,<br />
to witness it with my own eyes is like listening<br />
to your favorite part of a song<br />
or my favorite Bollywood songs<br />
with its catchy melodious lines.

If your face should be a landscape in its entirety<br />
and your lips, then, is its poetry.
