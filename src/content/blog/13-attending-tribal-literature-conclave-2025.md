It was a literary phenomenon,
the state government sponsored event
and organized by Paite Literature Society
and Paite Laipau Saipawl.
It was almost a miracle like—  8 languages
of the land: Gangte, Hmar, Mizo, Paite, Simte,
Thadou, Vaiphei, Zou, and their literature society
representatives sat on a round panel,
discoursing by speaking their own mother tongue
but remained perfectly intelligible to each other.

And as I listened to the beautiful cacophony
of the land tribal languages, I became more
and more dazzled, and after an hour
I couldn't distinguish which one was which,
and so I said to myself:
This must be how Heaven is.
Multiple tongues in one harmonious place.

Only that, I left the event earlier than I anticipated
because in the Tribal Literature Conclave 2025
the most heated talk was on the alphabets
of the attending languages and the rudimentary
of how to write them correctly.
