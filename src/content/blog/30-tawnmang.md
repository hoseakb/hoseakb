In Hmar, night dreams that come to you
when you sleep is simply 'Mang', but in poetry
one way of saying it is 'Tawnmang',
'tawn' to experience, encounter. So, 'Mang'
is not simply a dream.
It is an experience you encounter
on the other side
of your day.

And you know how it is,
there are mornings when you wake up
with a certain dream and its after-smoke
heavily lingers on your eyelid long after.

Maybe this is why when you say goodnight
traditionally in Hmar, you say "Mangṭha",
bidding good dream.
Maybe because I slept last night with your
before sleep 'Mangṭha', my 'Mang' was good,
and now I want to see you again. I want you.
I want to sit beside you. I want you. I yearn.
