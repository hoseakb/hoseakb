When I first witnessed this valley in 2006
I was just a dreamy boy from Parbung...
and this town in all its grandeur
with the firefly infested flowing river Tuiṭhapui,
this town, this place— from that vantage point
in Pearsonmun Peak— it was a fairyland,
firefly illuminated valley of my old dreams,
the founding place of my childhood dreams,
the 'Phaipui'  of my grandmother's old time stories.
And now that I am here, trying to build a house,
trying to earn a living, I don't know anymore...
whether I am living a daydream or a nightmare.

But then I found you, I found you in this town
of many names— I found you, I found you
and suddenly, you have made this town, this town
of many names, this town of many conflicts,
a bit hospitable— a possible place of my dreams. the old days,
it is catharsis.
