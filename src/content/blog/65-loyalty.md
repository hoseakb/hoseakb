Loyalty is sexy. And beautiful.

In the world of water surface,
Swan and Geese mate for life.
And to know them is to know that
if one dies, the other would die right after
instead of mating with another.

In the world of the endless sky,
Albatrosses mate for life.
No matter how far and long the journey
is, albatross always return to their nest,
to their home, if not... the other pair just die.

In the marine world,
Seahorses mate for life.
And male seahorses would have been
the favourite of feminist: He gets pregnant,
gives birth, and looks after the babies.

In the human world,
there is you, and me.
