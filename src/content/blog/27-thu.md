'Thu'  is a word in Hmar, my mother tongue.
And it means— first of all, the Word.
As in John 1:1-3, "In the beginning..."
A narrative of anything. Poetry. Folk songs.

Second, it also means 'a pungent smell',
from fart, shit, etc... to any other thing
that smell unpleasant.
Third, it also refers to the four main
Hmar kitchen curry condiments— sathu, sithu,
ngathu  and bekanthu. Traditionally fermented
pork lard, sesame seeds, fish, and soyabean.

I come from a world where just one lexical word
'thu'  can mean almost everything I love
about my culture. And so, why wouldn't my taste,
and my sensibility, should not be any different?

'Thu', therefore, is the beginning of everything,
and shall be the last. It can create, build,
and destroy.
