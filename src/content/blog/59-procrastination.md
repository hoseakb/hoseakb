It has another name:
negligence's  excuse

In college, I justified it:
"My mind works exceedingly well
under a deadline's last minute pressure."
In university, it became a lifestyle.

The other day, in my lucid after-morning
dream I was chased by a python
in the old garden behind my house in Parbung.
It is like those dreams in which
you are being chased but cannot run.
The big snake was just behind my heel, hissing
but I couldn't run, nor move! I woke up,
whimpering and hyperventilating
and I knew— the big snake was all the pending
works I left behind on schedule
and called it procrastination.
