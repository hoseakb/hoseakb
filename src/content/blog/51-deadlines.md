Deadlines are my enemy, always.<br />
I mean, I am bad at keeping at it:<br />
2 out of the 16 assignments, I remember<br />
it distinctly, I only ever managed to submit<br />
on time during my University days.

However, this morning,<br />
my mother, sitting infront<br />
of me, on the dinning table, said:<br />
"Your father and I are not young anymore,<br />
if you have found your God given one,<br />
I think it's time to get married."

It's November, 2nd Week.<br />
And I have never been happy like this<br />
since forever. I would look at the senset,<br />
at first in fascination, but now, for its beauty.

Deadlines are poetic, especially for poets.
