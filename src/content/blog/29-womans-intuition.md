I have known about it from the best,<br />
my mother— with her, mostly it has been<br />
always about my father.

Some morning, she would wake up<br />
with her bad dreams, "Mang Sie"<br />
as she would call it. And whatever premonition<br />
carries by her dream, it would always, it would<br />
always come true to the dot.<br />
There are other instances when she would know<br />
exactly what's going on even if I avoid telling her.

And now that you belong with me, Ṭhamâmpui,<br />
would you be like my mother?<br />
Would your intuition be as spooky, knowing<br />
the weather of my daily landscape even when<br />
I do not have the courage to tell you out of inphalam?
