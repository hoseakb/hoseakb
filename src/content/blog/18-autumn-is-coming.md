It is one of those afternoons<br />
in late summer when sunset<br />
flirt with whatever room you are in<br />
through a curtained window<br />
in the way early autumn sunset flirt.

It is the last class of the day<br />
and my students are sweating out answers<br />
to a bunch of questions on Edgar Alan Poe.

The day has been sweltering<br />
and besides that, anything special is yet<br />
to happen still in my side of the world.<br />
And then, you send me a package of you,<br />
your thoughts, and the longing of your heart<br />
through internet. God bless the Internet!

And now I am yearning for you from where I am,<br />
the afternoon look of your face, your fragrance.
