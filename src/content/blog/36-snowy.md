Snowy is the name of my family cat.<br />
She is already one year and a few weeks old now.<br />
She was born out of love and adopted as such.

And my father, I never knew him<br />
as I do now with Snowy in the family.<br />
He opposed, at first, the idea of adopting a cat.<br />
Two kittens at that— Snowy and her sister Naoko.<br />
And now my father, he has become a changed man<br />
since the coming of the two kittens.<br />
When Naoko stopped coming home last November,<br />
he was heartbroken, ready to raise loud commotion<br />
in the neighborhood.

My father, who never showed his soft side,<br />
to me and my sister, has been all soft and talkative<br />
with Snowy. He would hold her, cajole her, and pamper her.<br />
And Snowy too, has grown attached to him—<br />
she would always go to my parents' bedroom, looking<br />
for her favourite person in the family to sleep.

I never really knew my father before<br />
and it took me a sweet kitten in the family to really<br />
know him. Maybe he wants grand kids now.
