There exists a larger world inside me,
way larger than the physicality of myself,
the actual extent of which I do not know.
And this world that only stays within the confine
of the interiority of my conscious
and subconscious mind only speak
and dream in Hmar, the language of my birth.

On that December night when I was born
my mother sang me to sleep with her native lullaby
of a brooding hen which she learned from her mother.
And my father, same as the rest of my maturing life,
remained drunkenly silent throughout the night.
Only later in life did I get to find out that
he was like that only because he had always been
half illiterate. From there, from that night
I was born I began learning how to speak,
how to articulate myself to the rest of the world.
First, to the ashes of my mother's traditional hearth.
And only then did I learn how to decipher the wordings
in my father's silence.

And now, even though I have spent all my life learning
how to find poetry in the language of this poem,
I shall always speak best and most intimately,
in the language of my interior world.
That is, Hmar, the language of my birth.
