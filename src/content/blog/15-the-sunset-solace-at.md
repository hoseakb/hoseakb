I do not know when you become
the quiet peace-giving part of my day,
the sunset solace at the end of my day,
but you do indeed, and you have become
the one place I run to whenever I need
to get away from the noises... of my day.

I have not really known silence before
especially the peaceful kind. I grew up
in a typical Hmar household,
and the neighborhood... And more
noisier yet is the inside of my head,
and the kuduk kuduk beatings
in the hollow cavity of my chest,
and the never-ending market-like noises
of my mind. For instance, today
my day was draining and noisy,
so by afternoon, I was drained out
of clear eyesight and clear head.
And you, you were there...

You and the loud language you always
manage to speak with your eyes
and that whenever I get to sit beside you,
even on a drained out evening like today,
I would always feel like I am finally at rest
at long last, where I am peacefully supposed to be.
