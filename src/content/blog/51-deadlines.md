Deadlines are my enemy, always.
I mean, I am bad at keeping at it:
2 out of the 16 assignments, I remember
it distinctly, I only ever managed to submit
on time during my University days.

However, this morning,
my mother, sitting infront
of me, on the dinning table, said:
"Your father and I are not young anymore,
if you have found your God given one,
I think it's time to get married."

It's November, 2nd Week.
And I have never been happy like this
since forever. I would look at the senset,
at first in fascination, but now, for its beauty.

Deadlines are poetic, especially for poets.
