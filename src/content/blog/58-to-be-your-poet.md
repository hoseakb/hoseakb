That interview when Louise Glück talks about
what is poetry, and what it means to be a poet,
she said, "A poet is something you must aspire to
to be but cannot quite attain."
When someone asks, I always say:
"I am a practitioner of poetry. Not yet a poet."
And more so now, Toi, now that I have met you,
now that I finally have you in this lifetime,
I realize more and more
that I am nowhere near anything
a poet must be. Because you have become
my sole poetry, heart and soul, and that,
how shall I ever write one that talks about you
in ways true poetry talks real things into eternity?
