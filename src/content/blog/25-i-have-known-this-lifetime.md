I have known this lifetime
only as much as my own life has permitted me:
I have known Parbung, my ancestral village,
only as much as my childhood days had allowed me.
And the rest of the world outside of Parbung,
also only as much, limitedly and subjectively.

I have also been known to people
just as I have known them, limitedly and subjectively.

I have known this lifetime, only as much,
only halfway, only one way, only one sided.

And then, you happened.
You happen, and it's everything.
And so, now that I have known you,
I know this lifetime enough as much
as I should and must.
