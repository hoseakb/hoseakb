It has another name:<br />
negligence's  excuse

In college, I justified it:<br />
"My mind works exceedingly well<br />
under a deadline's last minute pressure."<br />
In university, it became a lifestyle.

The other day, in my lucid after-morning<br />
dream I was chased by a python<br />
in the old garden behind my house in Parbung.<br />
It is like those dreams in which<br />
you are being chased but cannot run.<br />
The big snake was just behind my heel, hissing<br />
but I couldn't run, nor move!

I woke up, whimpering and hyperventilating<br />
and I knew— the big snake was all the pending<br />
works I left behind on schedule<br />
and called it procrastination.
