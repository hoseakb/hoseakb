You question what I see in your face
and by extension, the way I see the world.
I reassure you that there is nothing extraordinary
about it. And that I just choose to talk about
only the bright and the beautiful that I see.
For example, you and your face.

Our world is old and large.
And we are too little and our lifetime,
too short. All things ugly and despairing
are surely enough for the rugged side
of everyday reality. And this place,
this winding river of me with you,
like my hopeless chattering poetry
that has been murmuring close to your ears,
and sometimes like a slanting rain,
at other times like a sun-shower of happy tears—
this place, your place, my place,
is where I come haunting you, again and again,
seeking to harvest happiness, joy, and comfort.
Bringing my wishes— my tribal narrative
and folklore for offerings in exchange.

If we were in the time of our ancestors'
ancient hills and mountains, I am afraid that
I would have already fallen at your feet,
swooning before the resplendent deity that you are,
proclaiming you the Chawngtinler  of my wilderness.

*As Hmar legends has it, Chawngtinler  is considered to be
the queen of all forest fae, wildlife, and the forest itself.
To get her boon is to become a successful hunter-warrior.
