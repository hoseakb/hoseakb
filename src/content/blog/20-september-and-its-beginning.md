There is this old sadness
in the old 2010s Bollywood songs
that, sometimes, comes crashing into me
like a childhood Parbung nostalgia.

I am on my way to work,
and to drown out the mad poetry
of this town morning traffic, I put on
my headphones. Just before I set off from home,
my mother got a phone call that shocked her
to tears— Nu Khuongdailo has passed away.

And all these, with the added heaviness
of Monday, of the new month, of the new season,
and the continued requirement to make a living
despite the dust of Muolvaiphei road
and the heat of the sun should have been enough
to crash me completely but you, you,
you have made it easy, you have made me...
you have made it... you, you have made it bearable.

Because with you in my life, even the heavy beauty
of September and its beginning does not scare me
for the first time.
