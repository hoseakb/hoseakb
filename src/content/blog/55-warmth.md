I would not know how the stars are<br />
tonight in the sky above my dusty<br />
shivering Muolvaiphei street.<br />
I am in my room, summered and without you,<br />
and in front of me are piles and piles<br />
of academic works.

So, Toi,<br />
this is me thinking of all the lovely things<br />
to get me through the night, dusty<br />
April February nights without you.<br />
Because the thoughts of you on my home<br />
is not you, nor the nightly<br />
on my legs, nor this December hoodie.

You must be here. You must be here.<br />
You need to be all the warmth in my nights<br />
because, now that you exist, no warmth<br />
is warm sufficiently enough, unless it's you.
