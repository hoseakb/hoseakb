Some zealots are sending me a message:<br />
‘Let us pray for Israel’.‍

With deep anguish, I think<br />
of our people’s Three Commandments,<br />
especially two:‍

Live in the knowledge of man and God.<br />
Earn your virtue.‍

I wonder if praying for Israel<br />
is living in the knowledge of man and God<br />
or earning my virtue.‍

I will certainly not pray for genocide;<br />
I will certainly not pray for ethnic cleansing;<br />
I will certainly not pray for the evil from hell<br />
unleashed upon the earth.‍

A great remorse washes over me;<br />
a great abhorrence overwhelms me<br />
that these—my people—<br />
should idolise devilishness for virtue.
