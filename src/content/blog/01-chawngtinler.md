You question what I see in your face<br />
and by extension, the way I see the world.<br />
I reassure you that there is nothing extraordinary<br />
about it. And that I just choose to talk about<br />
only the bright and the beautiful that I see.<br />
For example, you and your face.

Our world is old and large.<br />
And we are too little and our lifetime,<br />
too short. All things ugly and despairing<br />
are surely enough for the rugged side<br />
of everyday reality.

And this place,<br />
this winding river of me with you,<br />
like my hopeless chattering poetry<br />
that has been murmuring close to your ears,<br />
and sometimes like a slanting rain,<br />
at other times like a sun-shower of happy tears—<br />
this place, your place, my place,<br />
is where I come haunting you, again and again,<br />
seeking to harvest happiness, joy, and comfort.<br />
Bringing my wishes— my tribal narrative<br />
and folklore for offerings in exchange.

If we were in the time of our ancestors'<br />
ancient hills and mountains, I am afraid that<br />
I would have already fallen at your feet,<br />
swooning before the resplendent deity that you are,<br />
proclaiming you the Chawngtinler  of my wilderness.

*As Hmar legends has it, Chawngtinler  is considered to be
the queen of all forest fae, wildlife, and the forest itself.
To get her boon is to become a successful hunter-warrior.
