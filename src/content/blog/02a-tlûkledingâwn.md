It is a quantifying word in Hmar,
an equivalent for billion, a number
with 9 zeroes. And probably the least use
word in the lexical world of the Hmar,
my native tongue. Because my culture
has never been economically wealthy enough
to require the word on a daily.

The word 'Tlûkledingâwn'  is not only
dramatically useless but also hyperbolically
hilarious as in 'Tlûk'  for crashing down,
'le'  for and, and 'dingâwn'  for bending down
with weight.  Therefore, Tlûkledingâwn
dramatically and hyperbolically means
bending and crashing down under a great weight.

And so I estimate everything in my world
and also everything I have ever owned.
I compare my sum load of everything together
with your weight in my heart
only to find out that you are the only worth
equivalent to Tlûkledingâwn  that exist
in my world. You are my Tlûkledingâwn.
