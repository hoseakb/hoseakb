Eve, in that eternal paradise garden of Eden,<br />
surely linked up with the serpent before. Before,<br />
before that day she got entangled in the webs<br />
of Satan. She knew him before. She must have.<br />
Satan must have been her ex, in all honesty.

Haunted house. It's scary.<br />
It's the one place almost all horror movies<br />
take place. Anything, a place, or a person<br />
that is haunted by the ghost of the past,<br />
the old past that is supposed to be bygone,<br />
 it's scary. It's toxic. It's damaging.

Adam, that time when his wife Eve<br />
betrayed him with the serpentine Satan,<br />
even though he knew about it all clearly,<br />
because he loves his wife Eve<br />
and that he thought he couldn't live<br />
without her, without her he is not Adam,<br />
and so he ate the fruit of eternal damnation.<br />
Because it's either Eve or eternal loneliness?

And the horror of the aftermath,<br />
it's scary. It's toxic. I fear, heart-crashingly.
