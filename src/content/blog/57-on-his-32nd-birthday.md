He realises that now he is turning 33
He is turning 33 suddenly
and his mother says, 'You are not young
anymore'
There is talk of getting married too
Suddenly he is not 23 anymore
He has not been 23 for a long time now
but he doesn't feel 30. He has never felt
30.

For the first time in his 30 odd years
every year on the 8th of December
he doesn't think too much about
his twin brother.
