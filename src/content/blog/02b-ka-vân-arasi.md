Vân arasi en ka thiem hma chun<br />
ka vânlaizawlah tlep ter tuorin<br />
an de êr uor khawmin arasi pangngai,<br />
ka tadinga umzie neilo el an ni hlak.<br />
Arasi hming sûta ka ko thiem hung ruok chun,<br />
bawmkhata inlawi arasi hai lem chuh<br />
arasi pangngai el an ni ta nawh.<br />
Hming lama ka ko thei, zân khawvaiah<br />
vân ka en chânga ka mel kai ruoi hlak<br />
arasi hai an ti ta si leiin.

Famta Kama Sungte thangvânsânga khan<br />
siar ruol ar ang indiin an leng hmun a.<br />
Keikhawm ka vânlaizawlah siar lai sîngin<br />
ropui i rel ta zing.<br />
Arasi dang chu hril lo, ka khawvela hin<br />
i var chiengkuong êm ta lei hin vân thengreng<br />
po po khawm i var ênga chu thil narân,<br />
an hming khawm ka hriet lo,<br />
ka hriet nuom bawk lo,<br />
ka mitsir khawma ka hmu fuk nuom lo,<br />
thil pangngai an inchang zo ta.

Sizucho angin ka lungril i cho thar zing a.<br />
Siruk angin ka cham lovin i mi lak ta.<br />
Ka Hrangsuon tadingin Chawngmawi i ni a.<br />
Zangkhuo angin ka lungril Hmar arasi<br />
i tinzawn zing hlak leiin.
