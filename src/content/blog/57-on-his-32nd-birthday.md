He realises that now he is turning 33<br />
He is turning 33 suddenly<br />
and his mother says, 'You are not young<br />
anymore'<br />
There is talk of getting married too

Suddenly he is not 23 anymore<br />
He has not been 23 for a long time now<br />
but he doesn't feel 30. He has never felt<br />
30.

For the first time in his 30 odd years<br />
every year on the 8th of December<br />
he doesn't think too much about<br />
his twin brother.
