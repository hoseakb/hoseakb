I hate past seasons,
especially the ones that find their ways
in the present season.

You had your fallout with someone else
before me. I understand, I had mine too,
especially with my father. And I hate it.
Because I am being compared to your past.
My father, he always compares me to others.

That's why I hate to know anything about
all the names in your past. It's a competition.
I don't like competition.
More than anything in my list of hate
is me knowing the contact number and name
of your past, in your phone log and life.
I hate it. I dread it. I don't... ever ever want it.
