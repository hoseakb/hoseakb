It's a website in which you'd be able to read
all my favourite essays (numbering 506 in total)
in Hmar and occasionally in English
by L. Keivom— the one Hmar poet whose poetry
has always been the Homer to my Virgil,
 the Virgil to my Dante, the Dante to my Milton.
L. Keivom, whose poetry sustained my Parbung days,
whose poetry was the only "...force that through
the green fuse drives the flowers...."
of my 2006s Parbung days.
I was just a school boy in that memorable RPC church
mission school named 'Jubilee Standard School'.

Now that it's September— 'Thlaram' in Hmar,
the month when everything decays or collapses
and I miss him, L. Keivom. I long for his new poetry.

This is not a poem eulogizing him though
because L. Keivom has been rendered eternal
within the confines of Hmar Literature.
And this poem, therefore, is rather because of
my hopeless wish for the number '506' to change
for a new update, a new article in his blog-page,
my guru L. Keivom.

L. Keivom has passed away in November 2021
and I cried like a baby in my University hostel room
for the first time ever in my life.
