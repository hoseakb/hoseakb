Snowy is the name of my family cat.
She is already one year and a few weeks old now.
She was born out of love and adopted as such.

And my father, I never knew him
as I do now with Snowy in the family.
He opposed, at first, the idea of adopting a cat.
Two kittens at that— Snowy and her sister Naoko.
And now my father, he has become a changed man
since the coming of the two kittens.
When Naoko stopped coming home last November,
he was heartbroken, ready to raise loud commotion
in the neighborhood.

My father, who never showed his soft side,
to me and my sister, has been all soft and talkative
with Snowy. He would hold her, cajole her, and pamper her.
And Snowy too, has grown attached to him—
she would always go to my parents' bedroom, looking
for her favourite person in the family to sleep.

I never really knew my father before
and it took me a sweet kitten in the family to really
know him. Maybe he wants grand kids now.
