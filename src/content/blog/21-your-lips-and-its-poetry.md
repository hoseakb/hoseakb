Your face is a landscape with its own raw poetry
in its entirety, with its own hills and running valley,
with its own moon phases of the zodiac seasons,
with its own rising and falling of weather in the sun,
and indeed, God must be very pleased.

Because your face is a landscape with its own pristine
Hmar poetry and I, I cannot be anything else now
except to be yours—to be your most devoted poet
for I want to make poetry out of it just as the moon
makes seasons out of a year's landscape.

And to witness your lips, naked or red lipstick stained,
your lips and its movement of opening or closing,
to witness it with my own eyes is like listening
to your favorite part of a song
or my favorite Bollywood songs
with its catchy melodious lines.

If your face should be a landscape in its entirety
and your lips, then, is its poetry.
