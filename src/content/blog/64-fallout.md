I hate past seasons,<br />
especially the ones that find their ways<br />
in the present season.

You had your fallout with someone else<br />
before me. I understand, I had mine too,<br />
especially with my father. And I hate it.<br />
Because I am being compared to your past.<br />
My father, he always compares me to others.

That's why I hate to know anything about<br />
all the names in your past. It's a competition.<br />
I don't like competition.

More than anything in my list of hate<br />
is me knowing the contact number and name<br />
of your past, in your phone log and life.<br />
I hate it. I dread it. I don't... ever ever want it.
