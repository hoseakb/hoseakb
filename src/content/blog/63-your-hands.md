Ab Samsui is a poet who writes
mostly in Hmar, my mother tongue.
In one of his poems, he writes about
the rustic beauty of his beloved's hands,
marred by an eldest daughter's responsibilities
and Pherzawl village jhumfield works.

Toi, while reading the poem I thought of
your right-hand thumb. The harden knuckle
right above your thumb because of your long-term
scissors handling. And your bones, subjected under
a constant constraint of deadline orders.

Toi, when I say, "You are pretty. (I hmel a thra.)"
I truly mean it along with the intricacies of the poetry
of all your designed-and-tailored dresses.
