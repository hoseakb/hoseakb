It takes me thirty three autumns,
or maybe a lot more, to finally find
the one, the woman with whom I want
to have children and create a family.
To create a family is nothing like
creating a poem. Especially
in this modern days, where
even merely existing is scary.

I am, and I was, a product
of my own mother's motherhood.
Even after a lifetime of MA degree
in English Literature, hundreds of lifestyle
magazines, novels, 4 epic poems,
hundreds of movies, the Internet,
Tolkien, Rowling, Mario Puzo, Pablo Neruda,
Mahmoud Darwish, Rainer Maria Rilke,
academic conferences, PhD research...
I am, and I am still the product of my mother's
Hmar motherhood. Her language becomes
my mother's tongue. And her love...
the only kind of love I might truly understand.
So, Ṭhamâmpui,
when I say I love you, I love you in Hmar,
with whatever my mother's traditional motherhood
has made me. That is, nakedly and with everything
I am in this life and on Earth, with everything
I shall ever be.
