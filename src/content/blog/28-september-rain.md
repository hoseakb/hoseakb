There is something in listening
to the gurgling of rain water
behind the window of your room at night.
It's September, and I don't remember
which and what day... because I only know you.
And it's raining right now, and outside
my window is a world where I will be drenched
if I ever go out. I miss you, I miss you
whenever it rains, I miss you in the rain,
at nights like tonight, like right now.

You have done what September rain
does to a night like tonight, like right now,
like the gurgling sound of a roof rainfall
behind my window, just like that, just like that
to me.
