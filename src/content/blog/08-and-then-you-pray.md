If I should begin from the beginning,
that is, way before you came to cup lovingly,
in your palm, my heart, with love
and tenderness— you see, even now
I don't know whatever that was that
I have done good in this life to deserve you...
So, I cannot just make myself believe that
I deserve this— I deserve you, simply—
that perchance I have done accidental good deeds
to deserve you.

And so, God tells me otherwise.
That— June will never be the same
and that the rain, the down-pouring rain,
in our Indian monsoon season
will never be the same. Because YOU
happened... So, whenever you pray, Ṭhamâmpui,
whenever you pray with me,
the angels who have been assigned for me
in Heaven always go crazy... Because
you are here with me on Earth.
And I know that you are here to stay
for a lifetime.
