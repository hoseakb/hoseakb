It's the last day of the month,<br />
October is ending. And there shall be<br />
no other day exactly like today<br />
and I need you, dearly. I need you<br />
as you were today. Now, here, now.

I mean, this is what my heart desires<br />
and against it<br />
is my sense of reality. I am old enough,<br />
mature enough to know that<br />
one doesn't always get one's desires.<br />
There had been days like today<br />
and there shall be more.

It's the ending day of October<br />
and the paddy rice field near my house<br />
are all harvested since yesterday.<br />
And I haven't seen your face today.
