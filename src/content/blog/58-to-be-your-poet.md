That interview when Louise Glück talks about<br />
what is poetry, and what it means to be a poet,<br />
she said, "A poet is something you must aspire to<br />
to be but cannot quite attain."

When someone asks, I always say:<br />
"I am a practitioner of poetry. Not yet a poet."

And more so now, Toi, now that I have met you,<br />
now that I finally have you in this lifetime,<br />
I realize more and more<br />
that I am nowhere near anything<br />
a poet must be.

Because you have become<br />
my sole poetry, heart and soul, and that,<br />
how shall I ever write one that talks about you<br />
in ways true poetry talks real things into eternity?
