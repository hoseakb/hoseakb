I singmit ka en chânga mi âikhuoptu,<br />
thukmeikhu inzâm chiei ang,<br />
zawldiai kai si lo khuoizu zûnzâm anga<br />
mi zêm lungzîngtu— ka lunglawm kimna dinga<br />
i sakhmel mawitu, i mitmeng mawi chunchang hi<br />
hril lang el lang, tlangkuo'n mi rel thang an ti'm?

En raw maw Ṭhamâmpui, a mak êm bawk a nih!<br />
Khawvel hi ka hmu ve phak chin a,<br />
dawng rawng rawng khawpin ka lo hmu ve ta hlak.<br />
Parbung khawvelin a huop chin, ka hmu thei<br />
le hriet thei chin lem chuh, tawnglo a muol neilovin<br />
kan hriet nawh. I sakhmel zêmtu i singmithai<br />
ka la hmu fuk mieu si naw a, khanglai khan.

Tun chu, i singmit ka hmu ta.<br />
Ka hmu hnung che a ka khawvel le a sunga um<br />
po po, ka en nuom zawnghai inthlakhleng dân hi<br />
hang ngaituo ve hrim la.<br />
Hmeltlailo i min hmu châng dam a lo um chun,<br />
ka khawvel thar, i singmit rorelna khawvel thar hi,<br />
a ngui ruoi nasa thei âwm, hril ding hâmhai leiin.
