Today this year 2026 is a Sunday
and I should be feeling holy and churchy
but it's been 1,096 days since 3rd May 2023
when I had to leave the capital city
of my home state in terror. I am not alright.
It's been 3 years
and the nightmares of 3rd May continue.

Many people died on May 3rd Manipur
and many more houses burnt, displaced,
and traumatized. Sometimes, I think
the grudges of their ghosts are haunting us
now.

3rd May, it's been a long long torture
and I condemn you!
