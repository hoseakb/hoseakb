Don't you know that I have found
traces of you, your solidness
in the appetizing taste
of my traditionally cooked,
in my winter pork curry, Naute?
Winter is going to be cold.

It's December
and tender mustard leaves are on sale
in the market, and ginger bulbs
and bamboo fermented shoot.
Come home soon, Naute.
Come home and let me cook you
the tastiest of pork recipes
I have learned how to cook.
