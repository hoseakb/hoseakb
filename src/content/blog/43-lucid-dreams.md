My dreams are outrageously funny<br />
sometimes. At other times,<br />
I would wake up to some particular dream<br />
still lingering heavily on my eyelids:

The other day, last month I think,<br />
I woke up to this terrorizing dream.<br />
I knew it was just a dream but<br />
for five minutes, in the first hour<br />
of that morning, I was paralyzed with<br />
this sickening, vomit inducing high temperature—<br />
in my dreams, I saw you riding around town<br />
with your ex. You were talking to him,<br />
texting him... In my dream.

You couldn't help yourself.<br />
I was not enough. You didn't love me enough<br />
to cut your ex off completely.

But by the afternoon of that day,<br />
when I saw your face... I knew that it was all<br />
a dream...that my dream of the night<br />
was just a dream, the psychological effects<br />
of fearing to lose someone you love so completely.
