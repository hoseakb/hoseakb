When I first witnessed this valley in 2006<br />
I was just a dreamy boy from Parbung...<br />
and this town in all its grandeur<br />
with the firefly infested flowing river Tuiṭhapui,<br />
this town, this place— from that vantage point<br />
in Pearsonmun Peak— it was a fairyland,<br />
firefly illuminated valley of my old dreams,<br />
the founding place of my childhood dreams,<br />
the 'Phaipui'  of my grandmother's old time stories.

And now that I am here, trying to build a house,<br />
trying to earn a living, I don't know anymore...<br />
whether I am living a daydream or a nightmare.

But then I found you, I found you in this town<br />
of many names— I found you, I found you<br />
and suddenly, you have made this town, this town<br />
of many names, this town of many conflicts,<br />
a bit hospitable— a possible place of my dreams.<br />
The old days, it is catharsis.
