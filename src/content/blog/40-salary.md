I want to blame Sir Adam Smith,<br />
and Karl Marx partly,<br />
for my 21st century economic awareness.<br />
And thus, this restless self dissatisfaction.

I earn 29 thousands Indian rupees per month<br />
with my job, this is 326.92 US dollar internationally,<br />
by working rain or shine daily for a month.<br />
And 80% of this salary vanishes before my payday<br />
just to barely sustain the need and basic requirements<br />
of modern day 2025 daily existence,<br />
even in a small town like where I live and love,<br />
and dream, and aspire.

I don't know what it is exactly and concretely<br />
but by being a salary-earning member of society,<br />
I feel like I have become less societal, less churchy,<br />
less passionate, less... less than anything<br />
whom I have always dreamed I ought to be.
