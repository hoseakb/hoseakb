"Ai"  is a river crab<br />
that can be found fat and tasty in autumn<br />
in this part of the world, in the hills, where I live.<br />
"Hmepawk" is the traditional style<br />
how we cook autumn crabs with paddy rice<br />
and green bean leaves. It's the taste of autumn.

There are dishes so dear to my taste palate<br />
that I just cannot explain how it tastes fully<br />
other than in my mother tongue, Hmar.

That's how I love you. No words<br />
in my dictionary, both English and Hmar,<br />
would ever do justice to how much,<br />
how completely, how fully
