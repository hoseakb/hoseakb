By now, you have become solid,<br />
that state of matters we learned about<br />
in our high-school chemistry class,<br />
that state of matters I like most—solid state,<br />
not in your state of air, nor liquid,<br />
but solid, tangible, touchable, feel-able.<br />
Because I like to feel it solidly what belongs<br />
with me.

And so, I don't know if you know this,<br />
but with you, I have questioned my status in life.<br />
I have questioned my social standing.<br />
I have questioned my life before you, all of it.<br />
Because now that you are here, now that it's life<br />
after you, life after I met you, this is finally,<br />
this is nothing short of whatever I have always<br />
prayed for.

It's the end of September and I know, I know<br />
but I am not anxious at all for the first time.<br />
Because you are here, finally.<br />
You are here with me.<br />
Autumn is beautiful, at long last, for me.<br />
Because of you.
